## Brand & Style

The design system is engineered for operational excellence, clarity, and rapid decision-making. It adopts a **Corporate/Modern** aesthetic with a lean toward **Minimalism**, stripping away unnecessary ornamentation to prioritize data density and legibility.

The brand personality is professional, reliable, and precise. It targets logistics managers, fleet operators, and data analysts who require a high-performance environment that remains comfortable during extended use. The UI should evoke a sense of organized efficiency—moving from the heavy density of traditional logistics software to a breathable, high-contrast digital workspace.

## Colors

The color palette centers on a "clean-room" philosophy. The primary surface utilizes a crisp white or ultra-light slate to maximize contrast. 

- **Primary (Azure):** Reserved strictly for interactive actions, progress indicators, and primary call-to-actions.
- **Secondary (Slate Navy):** Used for primary headings and critical interface text to ensure maximum readability.
- **Tertiary (Muted Slate):** Applied to secondary information, icons, and metadata.
- **Neutral (Ghost Slate):** Provides the background layer for the application, creating a soft distinction from white card elements.
- **Border:** A subtle, low-contrast stroke used to define structural boundaries without cluttering the visual field.

## Typography

The design system exclusively utilizes **Inter** for its systematic, utilitarian nature. The typeface is chosen for its exceptional legibility in data-heavy tables and small-scale labels. 

The scale is designed to be hierarchical and functional. Headlines use tighter letter spacing and heavier weights to anchor sections, while body text maintains a generous line height for long-form reading. Labels utilize semi-bold weights and slight tracking increases to remain legible even at the smallest sizes (11px-12px) often found in logistics dashboards.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system follows a rigorous **8px grid** to ensure consistency across all components. The layout uses a **Fluid Grid** model with a maximum container width of 1440px for desktop screens to prevent line lengths from becoming unreadable.

- **Desktop:** 12-column grid with 24px gutters and 32px side margins.
- **Tablet:** 8-column grid with 20px gutters and 24px side margins.
- **Mobile:** 4-column grid with 16px gutters and 16px side margins.

Vertical rhythm is maintained through standardized "stack" variables, ensuring that the distance between a header and a card is always twice the distance between internal card elements.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain a clean, professional appearance, the design system utilizes **low-contrast outlines** and **ambient shadows** rather than heavy skeuomorphism.

1.  **Level 0 (Flat):** Used for the main background (#F8FAFC). No shadows.
2.  **Level 1 (Raised):** Used for primary cards and content containers. These use a 1px border (#E2E8F0) and a very soft, diffused shadow (0px 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)).
3.  **Level 2 (Overlay):** Used for dropdowns, tooltips, and popovers. These feature a more pronounced shadow (0px 10px 15px -3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1)) to lift them clearly above the content.

Depth is used sparingly to direct attention; the primary content should feel integrated into the page, not floating unnecessarily.

## Shapes

The shape language is defined by a **Rounded (8px)** corner radius, balancing professional rigidity with modern approachability. 

- **Small Components (Buttons, Inputs, Tags):** 0.5rem (8px) radius.
- **Medium Components (Cards, Modals):** 1rem (16px) radius.
- **Large Components (Sections):** 1.5rem (24px) radius.

This consistency reinforces the "precision" aspect of the brand, avoiding the playfulness of fully circular pill shapes while moving away from the harshness of sharp corners.

## Components

### Buttons
Primary buttons use the Azure background with white text. Secondary buttons use a Slate Navy outline or ghost style. Buttons have a fixed height of 40px for standard actions and 32px for compact toolbar actions.

### Input Fields
Inputs feature a white background with a 1px #E2E8F0 border. On focus, the border transitions to Azure with a subtle 2px glow. Labels are always positioned above the input for clarity.

### Cards
Cards are the primary container for data. They use a white background to pop against the #F8FAFC neutral surface. They include a subtle border and Level 1 elevation.

### Chips & Status Indicators
Status indicators use a semi-transparent version of the semantic color (e.g., 10% opacity Green for "Delivered") with bold centered text. They use the same 8px corner radius as buttons.

### Lists & Data Tables
Tables are designed for high density. Rows have a 48px height with a 1px bottom border. Header rows use a light gray tint (#F1F5F9) to distinguish from the data.

### Progress Bars
Utilize the Azure primary color for active states, with a light gray #E2E8F0 track. Used for tracking shipment progress and fleet capacity.
